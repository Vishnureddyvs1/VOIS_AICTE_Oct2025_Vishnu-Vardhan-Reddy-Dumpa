# 🏨 Airbnb Hotel Booking Analysis  
**AICTE VOIS Internship Project – October 2025**  

## 📌 Problem Statement  
Airbnb has changed the way people travel and stay, especially in big cities.  
But this rapid growth has also created challenges:  

- Certain neighbourhoods are overcrowded with listings.  
- Pricing varies drastically and creates imbalance.  
- Guest experiences remain inconsistent.  

This project explores **Airbnb Open Data** to uncover patterns in listings, hosts, reviews, pricing, and availability, aiming to bring clarity to these issues.  

## 🎯 Project Goals  
Our objective was to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to understand how Airbnb operates in urban areas and what insights can be drawn for both hosts and guests.  

**Key Objectives:**  
- 🔹 Clean and preprocess raw Airbnb data.  
- 🔹 Analyse listings distribution by neighbourhood groups.  
- 🔹 Identify top-performing hosts and their listing dominance.  
- 🔹 Compare reviews, availability, and pricing patterns across regions.  
- 🔹 Build compelling **visualisations** (bar charts, heatmaps, scatter plots, word clouds).  

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- **Python** – Data cleaning, wrangling, and analysis.  
- **Pandas & NumPy** – Handling and transforming datasets.  
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualisation and insights.  
- **WordCloud** – Text-based visualisation of listing names.  
- **Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab** – Development environment.  
- **GitHub** – Project version control and hosting.  

## 📊 Results & Insights  

### 🔹 Listings by Neighbourhood Group  
- **Manhattan & Brooklyn** dominate the market.  
- **Staten Island** has the least activity.  

### 🔹 Top Hosts  
- A few hosts manage **hundreds of listings**, highlighting market concentration.  

### 🔹 Review Patterns  
- Guest reviews are more frequent in central locations.  
- **Review activity ≠ Price** (weak correlation).  

### 🔹 Pricing Trends  
- Most listings cost **under $200/night**.  
- Luxury listings create skewed high-price outliers.  

### 🔹 Room Type Analysis  
- **Entire homes/apartments** are most common and expensive.  
- **Shared rooms** are rare but the cheapest.  

### 🔹 Availability  
- Many listings are either **365 days available** or **not at all**.  

### 🔹 Geographical Distribution  
- Dense clusters of listings in **Manhattan & Brooklyn** (map visualisation).  

### 🔹 Correlation Heatmap  
- Stronger link between **reviews per month & total reviews**.  
- Price shows little correlation with review activity.  

### 🔹 Word Cloud of Listing Names  
- Popular terms: *“Cozy”*, *“Private”*, *“Apartment”*, reflecting common marketing styles.

## 🚀 Conclusion  
This project provides a **data-driven overview of Airbnb’s urban operations**.  
We identified how certain neighbourhoods are saturated, how a handful of hosts control large parts of the market, and how guests engage differently depending on location and property type.  

Such insights can help:  
- 🏠 **Hosts** optimise pricing and availability.  
- 🌍 **Airbnb** manage overcrowding and improve user experience.  
- 📊 **Analysts & Researchers** understand market behaviour for policy and strategy decisions.
